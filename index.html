✦ Friday

A personal voice assistant for one person, running on his own laptop and phone.

Friday is a private, non-commercial assistant built for and used only by its owner, Shabbir Hussain. It answers his questions, keeps his reminders, and, when he connects them, reads his own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive so it can tell him about his day, find his files and draft or send messages he approves.

It is not offered to the public and has no sign-ups. Nobody else's data is used.
