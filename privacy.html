Privacy policy

Friday · last updated 12 October 2026

Friday is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Shabbir Hussain. This page explains what happens to Google account data when he connects his Gmail, Google Calendar or Google Drive to it.

What Friday accesses

Only the Google services he chooses to connect: Gmail (to read, search, draft and, when he confirms, send mail), Google Calendar (to read and, when he confirms, change events) and Google Drive (to find, read and download his files), plus his email address to show which account is connected.

How it is used

The data is used only to answer his own requests, such as a morning briefing or "what did Kishore send". It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models. Friday's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it goes

Sign-in tokens are stored only on his own laptop. To answer a request, the relevant content is sent to the AI model provider that powers Friday (Anthropic's Claude), which processes it to produce the answer. Nothing is kept on any server run by Friday; there is none.

Removing access

Each connection can be removed at any time from Friday's settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
